Privacy Policy — MyGoal

Effective date: 2026-05-07. Maintained by Vinnie Lata. Contact: vinnielata@gmail.com.

Summary

MyGoal is a 100% offline personal finance app. Your transactions, wallets, goals, photos, and settings stay on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data.

What stays on your device

  • Transactions, wallets, goals, and category assignments (in a local SQLite database)
  • Optional photos you attach to expense entries (referenced from your device's photo library)
  • App preferences (currency, weekly notification time)

What we collect

Nothing. MyGoal makes no network requests for analytics, advertising, or syncing. There are no user accounts.

Permissions and why we ask

  • Photo library — only when you tap "attach photo" while logging an expense.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on the weekly Sunday summary in Settings. Notifications are scheduled and delivered locally by the OS.

Your data, your control

You can export your full data anytime as CSV or JSON from Settings → Export. Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data.

Children

MyGoal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
